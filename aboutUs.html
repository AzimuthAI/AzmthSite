Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Azmth Terminal Pte. Ltd (“Azmth”, “we”, “us”) explains here how personal information is handled when you visit this website, request Early Access, or contact us. This policy covers our public informational website. Separate notices may apply when you register for or use Animas, Mikoshi, or another product.

1. Information we collect

When you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. When you submit our Early Access form, we receive the answers and contact details you provide through Google Forms. Please provide only information relevant to your request, and do not send passwords, wallet private keys, or sensitive identity documents through these channels.

Our hosting and service providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, and access times to deliver their services, maintain security, and diagnose errors.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, evaluate and manage Early Access requests, communicate about access you requested, discuss partnerships, maintain the website, and meet applicable legal obligations. Where consent is required, we seek it before using your information for an additional purpose, including optional marketing.

3. Google Forms and YouTube

Early Access buttons open a Google-hosted form. Information submitted there is processed by Google as well as made available to us as the form operator. The Mikoshi page also loads an embedded YouTube player. Loading or interacting with it can send technical information to Google, which may use cookies or similar technologies and associate activity with your Google account depending on your settings. See the Google Privacy Policy for Google’s practices and available controls.

4. Cookies and your choices

The website itself does not currently include a first-party advertising or analytics tracker. Third-party services, including the YouTube player and Google Forms, may use cookies or browser storage. You can manage these through your browser and Google account settings. Blocking them may affect embedded videos or form functionality.

5. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers supporting hosting, email, forms, and business administration, or when required by law or necessary to address security incidents or protect legal rights. We limit disclosures to the relevant purpose.

Providers may process information outside your country, including outside Singapore. Where applicable, we require overseas processing to meet the protections required by Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only while needed for the purposes described here or for legal or legitimate business requirements, then delete it or remove identifying details. We take reasonable measures to protect personal information; no online transmission or storage method is completely secure.

7. Your privacy requests

You may contact us to request access to or correction of information we hold, ask about its use or disclosure, withdraw consent, or request deletion. Requests are subject to applicable law and any necessary retention requirements. We may need to verify your identity. Withdrawing consent may prevent us from handling an Early Access request or continuing related communications.

8. External websites

Links to Animas, LinkedIn, X, and other websites lead to services with their own privacy notices. This policy does not govern their independent collection or use of information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The latest version and its update date will appear here. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact Azmth Terminal Pte. Ltd at noone@azmth.ai with the subject “Privacy Request”.
