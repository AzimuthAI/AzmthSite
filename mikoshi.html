PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: 1 Oct 2026

Azmth Terminal Pte. Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and financial information you share with us through our website www.azmth.ai and related financial services (collectively, the "Services").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

Because we provide financial technology services, we collect various categories of information necessary to deliver our services, satisfy identity verification requirements, and comply with law:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full legal name, date of birth, Social Security Number or tax identification number, government-issued photo ID (e.g., driver's license or passport), physical address, email address, and phone number.
  • Financial & Transactional Data: Bank account numbers, debit/credit card details, payment history, transaction records, account balances, and credit score or history (where applicable).
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referral sources collected via logs and cookies.
  • Know Your Customer (KYC) / Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Data: Employment status, source of funds, investor accreditation details, and background verification data required by financial regulations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly for legitimate legal, operational, and regulatory purposes, including:

  • Service Delivery: Processing transactions, managing your account, executing payments, and maintaining ledger records.
  • Identity Verification & Compliance: Verifying your identity, conducting fraud prevention checks, and complying with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) mandates.
  • Security & Risk Assessment: Detecting, preventing, and responding to cyber threats, unauthorised transactions, or fraudulent activities.
  • Customer Support: Responding to inquiries, resolving account issues, and sending service updates or transaction receipts.
  • Legal Obligations: Complying with court orders, subpoenas, regulatory inquiries, and audit demands.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share your information only under the following circumstances:

  • Financial Service Partners & Banking Partners: Bank partners, payment processors, card networks, and clearinghouses involved in completing your requested financial transactions.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors providing web hosting, identity verification, fraud detection, customer analytics, and customer support services, operating under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal and Regulatory Authorities: Government bodies, law enforcement, or regulatory agencies when required by law, regulation, or court order.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of company assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

4. Data Protection & Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal and financial information, including:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Strict multi-factor authentication (MFA) and role-based access controls for internal data access.
  • Compliance with Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI-DSS) where payment processing occurs.

Please note: No internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal and transactional information for as long as your account remains active or as required by financial regulations. Many financial laws require us to retain records (such as transaction history and KYC records) for a minimum of [e.g., 5 to 7 years] after account closure.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, GLBA), you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of personal data, subject to mandatory statutory retention obligations under financial regulations.
  • Marketing Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from promotional communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting support.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to ensure core website functionality, analyze web traffic, and secure your session. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings, though disabling essential cookies may impact web functionality and access to your account.

8. International Data Transfers

If you access our Services from outside Hong Kong & Singapore, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in jurisdictions where our servers or third-party providers are located. We take steps to ensure appropriate data transfer mechanisms are in place.
